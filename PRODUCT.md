# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Teenage finance learners, students learning advanced valuation and investment analysts, served by guided and advanced views (confirmed by the user).
## Product Purpose
A CFA-oriented investment research and valuation workbench, retaining CFP suitability as a separate discipline. Connect evidence, calculations, model challenges and visual exploration.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing Python/FastAPI engine, REST and MCP interfaces, document exports, and a plain browser interface. The example company and assumptions are fictional. Source-grounded workflows do not constitute a trained economist or actuarial certification. Licensed curricula are not bundled.
## Product Principles
Every material assumption is inspectable. Calculations are deterministic and tested. Uncertainty and unsupported methods remain visible. Guided explanations and advanced controls share one calculation engine.
## Visual commitment
The user explicitly selected a GitHub-style dark workspace: compact navigation and controls, sans-serif typography, editable financial tables and green/red candlestick colors. Build the working interface directly. Use original Valuation Lab branding, plain financial labels and no code editors for normal calculations.
